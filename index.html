Questions, cautions, warnings, and tips

Periodically the practical instructions will be interrupted by a blue question box like the one below. Please take a few minutes to consider the question inside, either on your own or by chatting with people around you.

This will help you get the most of the practical exercises and is the best way to prepare for your coursework.

Question: What pattern do you expect to see in this data? What might be causing this pattern?

Other times you will see an amber or red box with a warning to either pay attention to or avoid something.

Attention! Be careful about how you do this thing.

Warning! Do NOT do this terrible thing.

Finally, sometimes you will see green boxes with helpful tips or other important information.

Important! Here is a helpful tip.
